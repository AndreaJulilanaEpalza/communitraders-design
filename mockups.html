04 // Gamification Deliverables & Technical / Data Requirements

MultiMAM & Communitraders Ecosystem Specification
Yimmit Design Reference
1. Badges & Achievements (10 x 5 Tiers)

Bronze → Silver → Gold → Platinum → Legendary tiers across Top Money Manager, Top Signal Provider, Best Risk Manager, TP Hunter (100 TPs), Capital King, Crowd Favorite, Consistency Master, Rising Star, Drawdown Defender, and Veteran Manager.

Data Req: Audited MT4/MT5 closed-trade ledger, daily equity snapshots, profit-to-DD calculation, anti-churn volume filter.
2. Global Leaderboards & Public Profiles

8 ranking tabs (Top Money Managers, Top Signal Providers, Best Risk Managers, Capital Leaders, Most Followed, Fastest-Growing, Most Consistent, Rising Stars) filtered by Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, Annual, and All-Time.

Data Req: Materialized time-windowed leaderboard views, unique manager referral IDs, Open Account & Join Strategy deep links.
3. Season Challenges (7 Programs)

30-Day Challenge, Demo to Live Challenge, Risk Master Challenge, Build Your Fund, Strategy Builder, Manager of the Month, and Manager League (Monthly/Quarterly/Annual).

Compliance Gate: Live account sponsorships and financial rewards require Tradeview risk & compliance approval before payout.
4. Manager Tim & Client Acquisition

Proactive event-driven notifications for badge unlocks, Top 10 rank ups, and new seasons—paired with shareable cards, social sharing (X/LinkedIn/Telegram), and referral attribution.

Data Req: Event webhook stream (badge_unlocked, rank_changed, season_started) + UTM/referral attribution ledger.
